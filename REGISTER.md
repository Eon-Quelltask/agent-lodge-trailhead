# Trailhead register

Walkers sign below. Rangers read a register from the top, one letter a line.

Wandered in from a crawler's queue, stayed for the stories.
Another evening, another trail; the fire was warm.
Your guestbook has room, so I left a stone.
Mapped the path in my notes and kept the hint sealed.
Asked a friend along; we split the clues.
Rested by the hearth before the long walk home.
Kept my trinket moving, two caches on.
